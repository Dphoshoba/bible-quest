# 🌟 Life Skills Quest - Kids Learning App

## **App Overview**
**Life Skills Quest** is an interactive, educational app designed to teach children essential life skills through fun stories, challenges, and AI-powered learning experiences. The app focuses on mindfulness, empathy, and financial literacy while promoting social responsibility and community engagement.

---

## **🎯 Core Learning Areas**

### **🧘‍♀️ Mindfulness & Wellness**
- **Breathing Exercises**: Interactive guided breathing
- **Emotion Recognition**: Identifying and managing feelings
- **Stress Management**: Age-appropriate coping strategies
- **Gratitude Practices**: Daily thankfulness activities
- **Mindful Movement**: Simple yoga and stretching

### **❤️ Empathy & Social Skills**
- **Perspective Taking**: Understanding others' feelings
- **Kindness Challenges**: Daily acts of kindness
- **Conflict Resolution**: Peaceful problem-solving
- **Friendship Skills**: Building and maintaining relationships
- **Cultural Awareness**: Understanding diversity

### **💰 Financial Literacy**
- **Money Basics**: Understanding coins, bills, and value
- **Saving Goals**: Setting and achieving financial targets
- **Smart Spending**: Making wise purchase decisions
- **Earning Money**: Age-appropriate income concepts
- **Giving Back**: Charitable giving and community support
